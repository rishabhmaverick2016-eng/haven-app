# 🌿 Haven — A Personal Sanctuary for Your Wife

> **Built with love to replace overpriced, generic wellness subscriptions like Liven with a 100% free, private, shame-free sanctuary.**

---

## 💡 Why This Breaks the Cycle (Where Commercial Apps Fail)

Traditional habit apps and commercial tools often fail someone struggling with **overwhelm, ADHD freeze, and burnout** because:
1. **They demand high willpower when dopamine is already depleted.**
2. **They use shame mechanics:** Broken streaks, red badges, and guilt triggers that cause users to abandon the app after one off day.
3. **They lock basic psychological support behind \$50–\$100/quarter subscriptions.**

**Haven does the opposite:**
* **Zero Guilt, Zero Broken Streaks:** No streaks that reset to zero. Days of care are collected as glowing memories.
* **Energy-Adaptive:** If her battery is at 15%, her goal is *permission to rest and drink water*, not doing 10 habits.
* **Emergency Unfreeze in 60s:** An instant button when she's frozen on the couch or in bed.
* **100% Free & Private:** All data is saved on her phone's storage. No accounts, no data selling, no hidden fees.

## 🚀 How to Open It on Her Phone Right Now

### Option A: Open via Home Wi-Fi (Instant)
1. Double click `START_HAVEN.bat` on your computer (or run `python server.py`).
2. The terminal will show a link like: `http://192.168.1.4:8080`.
3. Open that link on her phone's browser (both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network).
4. **Install as App:**
   * **iPhone (Safari):** Tap the **Share** button at the bottom -> Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   * **Android (Chrome):** Tap the **3 dots** menu at top right -> Tap **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home Screen"**.
5. It will appear on her home screen with the Haven leaf icon and open full-screen just like an App Store app!

### Option B: Free 24/7 Cloud Hosting (Accessible Anywhere)
Because Haven is built with pure, self-contained modern HTML/CSS/JS, you can host it permanently for free on:
* **Vercel** (Drag & drop the `Haven` folder on [vercel.com](https://vercel.com))
* **GitHub Pages** (Push the folder to a GitHub repository and turn on Pages)
* **Netlify Drop** (Drag & drop the `Haven` folder onto [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop))

She can then access it anytime, anywhere, even when away from home!
